## Alloy sets

snaxcraft adds five smithing equipment families:

- **Hepatizon**
- **Shakudo**
- **Steel**
- **Electrum**
- **Adamant**

These are extra upgrade paths. Vanilla diamond-to-netherite smithing remains available.

Source: `dev/generated/matcha-flavoured-1.12/data/smithing_table/recipe/`
Source: https://modrinth.com/datapack/matcha-flavoured

## How to make them

Use a smithing table. Each recipe selects a vanilla base item, a template item, and an addition item to produce the corresponding alloy tool, weapon, or armor piece. Check the recipe book for the exact combination for the piece you want.

Examples include Steel from iron equipment and Adamant from diamond equipment. These are smithing recipes, not normal crafting-table recipes.

Source: `dev/generated/matcha-flavoured-1.12/data/smithing_table/recipe/steel_sword.json`
Source: `dev/generated/matcha-flavoured-1.12/data/smithing_table/recipe/adamant_chestplate.json`

## Set bonuses

Alloy pieces come with their own built-in enchantments: Shakudo regen, Electrum warding, Adamant divinity, Hepatizon riposte, Steel blast protection, and more. Some bonuses only kick in while you wear the full set.

Source: `dev/generated/matcha-flavoured-1.12/data/main/enchantment/`
