# snaxcraft alloy equipment

**Edition:** Java<br>
**Version:** 26.2 Paper<br>
**Last verified:** 2026-08-17

**Applies to:** snaxcraft server content

## Alloy sets

snaxcraft adds five smithing equipment families. These are extra upgrade paths; vanilla diamond-to-netherite smithing still works as normal.

| Set | Upgrades | Material you add | What it is good at |
|-----|----------|------------------|--------------------|
| **Hepatizon** | Copper gear | Phantom Membrane | Attack speed and movement |
| **Shakudo** | Copper gear | Shulker Shell | Passive healing while worn |
| **Steel** | Iron gear | Resin Brick | Explosions and blocking |
| **Electrum** | Diamond gear | Heart of the Sea | Harming nearby monsters, tool luck |
| **Adamant** | Diamond gear | Netherite Ingot | Absorption hearts, highest damage |

## How to make them

Alloy gear is made at a **smithing table**, not a crafting table. You cannot get these from a normal crafting grid, a furnace, or an anvil.

A smithing table has three input slots in a row and one output slot. Left to right, the inputs are:

1. **Template** (left slot) - for most alloy pieces this is just an ingot or gem, such as a Copper Ingot or a Diamond. A few pieces want a real Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template instead.
2. **Base** (middle slot) - the finished piece of gear you are upgrading, such as a Copper Axe or Diamond Chestplate. It must already be the tool or armor piece, not raw material.
3. **Addition** (right slot) - the material that decides which alloy you get, such as Phantom Membrane for Hepatizon or Netherite Ingot for Adamant.

Put all three in, then drag the finished piece out of the output slot on the right. Nothing is consumed until you take the result, so you can safely check whether a combination works. Order matters: the same three items in the wrong slots will not produce anything.

Each recipe lists **Ingredients** for those three slots. Every recipe image below shows those four slots in that same left-to-right order, with each slot labeled.

Source: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Smithing_Table

## What the bonuses do

Each piece lists plain **Stats** (damage, mining speed, armor, and so on). Most also carry effects, listed under **Comes enchanted with**.

Custom effects added by snaxcraft:

- **Divinity** (Adamant) - grants Absorption while equipped. It counts every Adamant piece you wear or hold, so more Adamant means stronger Absorption: one piece gives Absorption I, two gives II, three gives III, and a full set jumps to Absorption V. It refreshes on its own as long as the gear stays equipped.
- **Warding** (Electrum) - an aura that damages and slows hostile mobs within roughly 6 to 8 blocks of you, without you attacking. Wearing more Electrum makes it tick faster and hit harder, and a full set also grants Absorption. Mobs wearing copper armor resist it.
- **Shakudo Regeneration** (Shakudo) - periodically gives you Regeneration while worn. Each extra Shakudo piece shortens the gap between pulses, and a large amount upgrades it to Regeneration II.
- **Riposte** (Hepatizon) - when you hit a mob, you lunge backward out of range. It does not work while riding, gliding with an elytra, or in water, and it costs a point of durability per use.
- **Sanguine** - life steal: attacking heals you for part of the damage dealt.
- **Magic Protection** / **Greater Magic Protection** - extra armor against damage that normal armor ignores.
- **Cleanse** - clears harmful status effects off you while the armor is worn.

Vanilla enchantments such as Unbreaking, Protection, Blast Protection, Fortune, Silk Touch, Looting, Smite, Sweeping Edge, and Lunge behave exactly as they do in normal Minecraft.

Source: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Absorption<br>
Source: https://minecraft.wiki/w/Regeneration

### Set bonuses

Lines shown as **Set Bonus** only reach full strength when you wear the whole matching set. Partial sets still count - the effects above scale with the number of pieces equipped, so two Adamant pieces are better than one. Lines shown as **When Blocking** apply only while you are actively blocking, and **Per Equipment Bonus** lines apply once for each matching piece you have on.
