## Bread pipeline

1. Craft **3 wheat** together to make Flour.
2. Craft Flour with an egg or water bottle to make Dough.
3. Smelt Dough in a furnace, or cook it on a campfire, to make Bread.

Flour and Dough are their own items with their own look. Ordinary paper is not Flour or Dough. Steak still comes from smelting raw beef.

## Building and storage shortcuts

- Blast cobblestone or cobbled deepslate into gravel, then blast gravel into sand.
- Surround a chest with 8 leather to make a shulker box. The vanilla End recipe still works.
- Arrange calcite and raw copper in a 2x2 checker pattern to make 4 prismarine.
- Use 8 logs in a chest pattern to make 4 chests.
- Craft clay into cheerful or mournful clay statues.

## Kitchen quest ladder

The Kitchen branch starts with **Plant Wheat**, then walks through bread, steak, jam, curry, blasting, storage, prismarine, chests, and clay statues.
